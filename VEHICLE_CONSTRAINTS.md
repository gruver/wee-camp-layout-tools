## Field Guidance

- `Owner / Group`: Person, household, or group responsible for the vehicle.
- `Vehicle / Rig`: Van, truck, car, trailer, RV, box truck, or combined rig.
- `Approx Footprint`: Total parked footprint in feet (L x H from the raw
  notes, mapped directly to shape width x height).
- `Sleeps In Vehicle`: Always TBD in this dataset; see Derived Layout
  Assumptions above.
- `Can Detach / Move`: Whether truck/trailer can separate or vehicle can
  leave. Not stated in the updated raw notes; TBD for every row.
- `Arrival / Departure Needs`: Early arrival, late arrival, mid-event exit, or
  must remain accessible. Not stated in the updated raw notes; TBD for every
  row.
- `Placement Preference`: Quiet, social, kitchen-adjacent, shade-adjacent,
  frontage, edge, back, etc. Filled in based on vehicle size/role for this
  layout draft (compound and camper rigs are placed back-of-camp for quiet
  clustering; personal cars and team infrastructure are placed mid-camp).
- `Access Constraints`: Cannot be boxed in, needs door-side clearance, trailer
  ramp access, service-lane access, turning clearance, or water/kitchen
  access. Assumed service-lane access for all rows pending confirmation.
- `Notes / TBD`: Ambiguity, missing dimensions, assumptions to confirm.
